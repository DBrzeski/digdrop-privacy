Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Polska wersja poniżej

This policy explains how the mobile game Dig Drop (Android package com.fakeadgames.digdrop), published by FakeAdGames, handles your data. Contact: brzeski97@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect ourselves

None. Dig Drop has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your game progress and settings (level, coins, tools, sound, language) are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game. We do not receive, sell or share this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC (and Google Ireland Limited in the EEA and UK). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • the Android advertising ID and app set ID;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (model, operating system, language, screen size);
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic data (e.g. crash information).

This data is sent encrypted. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are personalised; without consent you may see non-personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders (for example about the daily reward) locally on your device. No data is sent to us for this. You can turn notifications off at any time in your Android settings.

4. Children

Dig Drop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

5. Your rights

Because we do not store your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side; you can remove all game data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the options above or Google's tools. If you live in the EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Poland: UODO). Questions? Write to brzeski97@gmail.com.

6. Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The current version is always on this page, with its date at the top.
