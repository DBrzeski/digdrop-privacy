Dig Drop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Polska wersja poniżej

This policy explains how the mobile game Dig Drop (Android package com.fakeadgames.digdrop), published by FakeAdGames, handles your data. Contact: brzeski97@gmail.com.

1. Data we collect ourselves

None. Dig Drop has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your game progress and settings (level, coins, tools, sound, language) are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game. The same applies to the age you enter on the age screen at the first start: it stays on your device and is used only to choose the right ad settings (section 2). We do not receive, sell or share any of this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC (and Google Ireland Limited in the EEA and UK). To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • the Android advertising ID (not for players under 13) and the app set ID;
  • IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device information (model, operating system, language, screen size);
  • interactions with ads (views, clicks) and diagnostic data (e.g. crash information).

This data is sent encrypted. The game is for all ages, so the ad settings depend on the age entered at the first start:

  • under 13: ad requests are marked as child-directed (COPPA) and under the age of consent (EU); Google shows only non-personalised ads suitable for all ages and does not use the advertising ID;
  • 13 to 15: ad requests are marked as under the age of consent (EU), so ads are not personalised; ad content is limited to teen-appropriate;
  • 16 and older: in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice any time under Settings → Ad settings in the game. Without consent you may see non-personalised ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

How Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders (for example about the daily reward) locally on your device. No data is sent to us for this. You can turn notifications off at any time in your Android settings.

4. Children

Dig Drop is suitable for all ages, including children. A neutral age screen at the first start asks for the player's age; for players under 13 the ads follow the rules for children described in section 2 (no advertising ID, no personalised ads, all-ages content only). We do not collect personal data from children ourselves. If you believe a child's personal data was collected, contact us and we will help to have it deleted.

5. Your rights

Because we do not store your personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side; you can remove all game data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage. For data processed by Google for advertising, use the options above or Google's tools. If you live in the EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Poland: UODO). Questions? Write to brzeski97@gmail.com.

6. Changes

We may update this policy when the game changes. The current version is always on this page, with its date at the top.
